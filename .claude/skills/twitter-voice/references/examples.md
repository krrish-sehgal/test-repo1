# Real tweets from the user, annotated

These are actual posts. Study the mechanics, not just the surface. When writing new tweets, match the *moves* these make — don't copy phrases from them.

---

## 1. Story / discovery arc

> recently texted @garvvvitt on WA for his guidance on how he's cracking ugc at kulfi
>
> i've been seeing a lot of reels on my feed, and saw people conversing about @kulfiapp in the comments
>
> when i checked the accounts, i was shocked to see they were all affiliated with kulfi

**Mechanics:**
- opens mid-story with a personal action ("recently texted"), no throat-clearing
- "on WA" — casual abbreviation, assumes the reader lives in the same world
- chronological: noticed → investigated → reveal
- "i was shocked" — genuine reaction stated plainly, not performed ("🤯 MIND BLOWN")
- names real people/brands with @handles — the scene is small and specific

## 2. Campaign recap with cliffhanger

> we recently did a linkedin campaign for @foldmoney_
>
> 25+ creators
> 2 weeks
> 25+ posts
> 5 millions+ impressions
>
> total cost = 1,25,500 INR (~$1300)
>
> can you guess how many new additions we had to our daily fold signups?

**Mechanics:**
- stats stacked one per line, no bullets, no labels beyond the bare minimum
- numbers are specific and unrounded (1,25,500 INR — Indian digit grouping, kept)
- USD conversion in parens for international readers
- "total cost =" — the equals sign, like a napkin calculation
- ends on a question, not the answer — the engagement is the point
- even the grammar slip ("5 millions+") stays; polish is not the goal

## 3. Taste / hot take

> biggest takeaway from this video, taste is returning
>
> less is more
>
> most launch videos try too hard to be nice
>
> this one just feels beautiful.

**Mechanics:**
- comma splice in the first line — types like she talks
- each line is a standalone aphorism; they escalate
- the ONLY period in the whole tweet is the final one — it lands the ending
- "feels beautiful" — emotional, simple vocabulary, no critic-speak

## 4. Quote-tweet one-liners

Reacting to a complaint about X's rebrand:
> i can explain

Reacting to her own earlier tweet asking @SpotifyEng to fix shuffle:
> nvm i switched to apple music

Reacting to a thread about kulfi's distribution:
> noice

**Mechanics:**
- the quoted post is the setup; the reply is only the punchline
- self-quote-tweeting her own complaint to deliver a twist ("nvm...")
- one word is a complete tweet when the word is right

## 5. Relatable joke

> if you see the process of how your favourite soya chaap is made, you'll never ever ever eat them

**Mechanics:**
- hyper-local reference (soya chaap) — never genericized to "street food"
- "never ever ever" — repetition as intensity, childlike on purpose
- no follow-up line explaining it

## 6. Casual win

> guys we got an internship offer🤣

**Mechanics:**
- "guys" — talking to the timeline like a group chat
- the win is framed as slightly absurd, not a humblebrag
- emoji glued to the last word, no space — phone-typed energy
- that's the whole tweet

## 7. Replies to other people's posts

Someone posts a "10 github repos to learn ai" list that ends:

> Don't bookmark all 10 and forget about them.
> Pick based on where you are: Python → Python-100-Days, ML → ML-For-Beginners, ... Agents → AI-Agents-for-Beginners
> Pick one. Build something. Then move to the next.

Three replies, three different moves:

**Sharpen (adds information the post didn't have):**
> small thing on the list, stable diffusion and segment anything are research code, not courses
>
> worth opening once you already know what you are looking for
>
> for actual beginners the microsoft ones and llms-from-scratch are the only real starting points here, the rest are destinations

**Take (stands alone, so it also works as a quote-tweet):**
> the last three lines are the post
>
> everyone reads a list like this, stars all ten, and feels like they learned something
>
> the repo you finish is the one you opened because you were stuck on something that week
>
> pick the problem first, then the repo.

**One-liner (the post is already complete, so just be the funniest thing under it):**
> starred all ten, will open zero, felt productive

**Mechanics:**
- none of them open with praise; the first line is already the addition
- the sharpen reply gives the author something to answer (defend the list), which is the point
- the take reply is a full thought, so it can be quoted instead of replied if the post is old
- none of them claim an experience the user hasn't had; a version like "llms-from-scratch is the one, [what you built]" needs the user to fill the slot

## Vocabulary notes

- slang that appears naturally: noice, nvm, guys, ngl
- "i" is always lowercase
- indian english constructions stay ("do the needful"-adjacent phrasing is fine if natural)
- money: INR with indian digit grouping, (~$X) conversion when the number matters
- @handles for people and companies in the scene
- emoji budget: 0 most tweets, 1 max, and only 🤣/😭-type self-aware ones
