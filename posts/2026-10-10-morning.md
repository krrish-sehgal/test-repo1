what the story is: Tesla dropped the "Full Self-Driving" name in Europe on 8–9 Oct 2026, renaming the system "Tesla Assisted Driving" after Germany's Federal Ministry of Transport called the FSD name "somewhat misleading". Tesla's German site no longer carries the old name and the old URL redirects; UK support pages use "Tesla Assisted Driving Basic". The rename came out of technical and liability meetings held with the ministry in September, in which the system was agreed to be allowed to exceed the valid speed limit by a maximum of ten percent, and Transport Minister Steffen Bilger said he would work to get the system approved across Europe ("On this basis I will now work to ensure that the system is approved across Europe soon"). The 6 Oct committee meeting discussed the Dutch request without voting, pushing a decision to December at the earliest; EU-wide approval needs 15 of 27 states representing 65% of the population. The rename and the 10% cap are not a national approval — Germany's KBA is still reviewing the file with the Dutch RDW — and the post says the vote is still to come rather than implying approval.
