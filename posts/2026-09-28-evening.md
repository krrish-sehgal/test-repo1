What the story is: a federal jury in the Southern District of California awarded Taction Technology
more than $5.72B on Friday 25 Sept, finding Apple's Taptic Engine infringed claims of US Patents
10,659,885 and 10,820,117, both covering vibration-based tactile transducer designs. Taction is a
San Diego haptics company whose Transporter technology powers haptic headphones and the Corsair HS60
Haptic gaming headset; the patents came out of research by Silmon James Biggs, PhD, now Taction's

infringement willful. It is reported as the largest US patent verdict to date, more than double the
