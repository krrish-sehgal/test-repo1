Cloudflare's mixed-use AI crawler policy, announced 2026-07-01 and effective 2026-09-15. Cloudflare
splits crawler behaviour into Search, Agent and Training; from 09-15 the defaults block Training and
Agent on pages that display ads, while Search stays allowed. Applies to all new domains onboarding,
new customers, and existing Free-tier customers, with an opt-out window that closed 09-15. Verified
against Cloudflare's own blog (primary), TechCrunch (07-01), Search Engine Journal, Search Engine
Watch, Playwire, Technology.org, MLQ and Windows Forum. The closing beat — Google runs a single
crawler for both search indexing and model training, so removing the Googlebot exemption is what
actually bites — appears in Search Engine Journal, Search Engine Watch and Cloudflare's own post.
Deliberately left out: the "USA Today, Reuters, Reddit and Politico are weighing a full Google
block" detail. It only surfaced in SEO trade coverage (Search Engine Watch, authoritytech) and I
could not get it to two genuinely independent outlets, so it was dropped rather than hedged.
`@Cloudflare` is safe. Google is named in the post but not tagged, since tagging the party the post
is critical of invites the wrong kind of reply thread.
