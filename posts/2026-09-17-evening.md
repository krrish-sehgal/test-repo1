What the story is: the Centre confirmed a 0.4% MDR on person-to-merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000
starting 15 Oct 2026, ending the zero-MDR regime in place since January 2020. Merchant-paid, capped
at ₹300 for transactions of ₹75,000 and above; essentials (railways, fuel, education fees,
government utility bills) capped at ₹5; payments up to ₹2,000 stay free, as do P2PM small merchants

of rollback". The closing stat is the strongest thing in the story: only ~4% of P2M transactions in
FY2025-26 were above ₹2,000, but that slice carried close to two-thirds of total UPI payment value;
UPI is ~88% of India's digital transaction volume at 24bn+ transactions a month. Verified against
BusinessToday (two pieces), Business Standard (two pieces), Onmanorama, Deccan Herald, TechTimes and
Vajiram & Ravi. Handle note: @NPCI_NPCI is NPCI's official account. @FinMinIndia would also fit but
the framework is the government's, not NPCI's alone, so one tag is safer.
