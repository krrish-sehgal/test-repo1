What the story is: on 25 Sept Akamai announced an $11.6B, seven-year contractual commitment from
Anthropic for CPU workloads on Akamai Cloud, with an option to expand by a further $9B to roughly
$20B. It is the largest contract in Akamai's history (founded 1998 by MIT's Tom Leighton and Danny
Lewin) and more than six times the $1.8B arrangement Bloomberg reported in May. As part of it Akamai
issued Anthropic a warrant for non-voting Series B preferred stock convertible into 7.7M common
shares, about 5% of shares outstanding, at $111.33 a share; roughly 2% is expected to vest on
Anthropic's first payment, the rest tied to further spend. Akamai expects about $5.5B of capex tied
to the deal, over six times its entire 2025 capital spend, adding ~$1.7B to this year's budget to
pre-buy components such as memory; first services are planned for late Q2 2027. Akamai's year-to-date
signed contract value is now about $14.4B. Verified against Akamai's own press release and its SEC
Form 8-K exhibit, TechCrunch, CNBC, Forbes, Quartz, Neowin, IT Pro and Benzinga, which agree on the
$11.6B, the seven years, the $9B option, the warrant terms and the $5.5B capex. The stock's move is

a number.

What the story is: on 25 Sept Dextr AI announced a $6.7M seed round, its first institutional
financing, led by Elevation Capital (the India-based fund, formerly SAIF Partners) with participation
