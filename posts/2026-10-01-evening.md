what the story is: two same-day AI governance events on 30 September. In Washington, Trump announced
a voluntary safety accord signed by him, Dario Amodei, Sundar Pichai, Mark Zuckerberg, OpenAI
president Greg Brockman, Jensen Huang and Elon Musk (xAI, now part of SpaceX); per the copy Trump
posted to Truth Social, signatories agreed to work with independent auditors on whether systems work
as designers intended and to ensure their tools do not "hack or access technical systems in
unintended ways". Trump called it "almost like a constitution". The same day Newsom signed 13 AI
bills covering workplaces, health care, online content and genetic research. SB 947, the No Robo
Bosses Act, bars automated systems as the primary driver of termination or discipline and requires a
human reviewer to corroborate using personnel files, manager evaluations and peer reviews, plus
written notice of the data used and a human contact; it is operative 1 July 2027 and is a reversal of
Newsom's October 2025 veto of the broader SB 7. AB 1331 restricts workplace surveillance tools and
bars monitoring employees in workplace bathrooms, with narrow exceptions for non-recording,
non-AI badges and personal alarms. SB 951 amends California's WARN Act so a technology-displacement
layoff notice must carry "This notice is for a technology displacement" at the top and name the
numbers, classifications and work locations affected, the job functions being automated, and the
specific category or type of AI system responsible; the state's EDD must post notice summaries and a
quarterly statewide technology-displacement report. The biometric emotion-inference ban comes from
AP's summary of the package. Only @GavinNewsom is tagged — a verified handle; no CEO or company
handle is tagged because the post is about the contrast, and tagging seven labs under it would read
