What the story is: from 15 October 2026, UPI person-to-merchant payments above ₹2,000 carry a 0.4%
MDR capped at ₹300, ending roughly a decade of zero-MDR UPI. Carve-outs: P2PM merchants collecting
under ₹1 lakh a month stay at zero; railways, telecom, insurance, fuel and agri inputs pay a flat ₹5
above ₹2,000; mutual funds, securities and stockbrokers pay 0.02% capped at ₹300. NPCI says small
merchants and 96% of transactions are unaffected. On Monday 28 Sept a Supreme Court bench of CJI
Surya Kant with Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V Mohana heard a challenge, refused any interim stay,
and issued notice to the Centre, RBI and NPCI for counter affidavits within four weeks — while
questioning who pays, who receives the money, what role RBI and NPCI play, and the legal character
of the charge (fee or tax). The closing line: on 11 June 2025 the Finance Ministry publicly rejected
reports of an MDR on UPI as "completely false, baseless, and misleading", saying there was no such
proposal; that post resurfaced after the new rules. Verified against Business Today, Al Jazeera,
India Legal, PTC News, Open magazine, Karmactive, India.com, NewsBytes, The420.in and YourStory for
the hearing and the framework, and against A2Z Taxcorp, CAclubindia, Tribune India, NewsOnAir and
