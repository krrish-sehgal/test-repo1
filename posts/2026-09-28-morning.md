What the story is: Gambit Security published research on 22 Sept after recovering a financially
motivated operator's staging server. Three open source agent harnesses did the work: Strix for
vulnerability discovery, Cairn for hours-long autonomous exploitation, Hermes for orchestration.
Between 10 and 15 Sept the operation created 105 attack projects and compromised at least 27
companies to varying degrees, among them a Fortune 500 hospitality company and a major US airline.
Over 600,000 unexpired card records were confirmed stolen; skimmers were confirmed on 19 targets with
100+ further related sites identified. Persistence included cron jobs that restored the skimmer after
removal. Per-target cost from the attacker's own records ran $3.13 to $79.31 across 101 completed
scans, averaging $25.46; an OpenRouter account showed $7,005.71 over about four weeks, with total
campaign cost estimated at $12,000–$18,000. Verified against BleepingComputer, The Register (25 Sept),
SecurityWeek, TechRadar, Cybernews, Quartz, Hackread, CyberSecurityNews and CybersecAsia, which agree
on the three agent names, the 27 companies, the 600,000 cards, the $25-a-company framing and the 1,951
prompts across 260 sessions.

**Two care notes.** The post says "over a hundred sites" rather than 119, because 19 skimmers are
confirmed and the rest are identified as related. The reporting that Hermes ran on a specific
commercial model, and the "SOUL – Red Team Operator" persona with 121 skills, are thinner sourced, so
both are left out of the post.
